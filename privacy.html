Privacy policy

Olaf’s Bubbles is an offline game for iPhone and iPad. The game does not collect or transmit personal or gameplay data. It has no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking.

Game preferences

The game saves your cat choice, fan setting, sound setting, and whether you’ve seen the fan tip on your device. It uses these preferences to remember how you like to play. The developer does not receive these preferences. To remove the preferences stored on your device, delete the app.

Support email

If you choose to email us, we use your email address and the details you share to respond and follow up. The developer’s email service handles and stores this correspondence. To ask about privacy or retention, or to request deletion of information you sent, email DavidGonzalez1224@gmail.com.
