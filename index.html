Playing the game

How do I change cats?
Tap Pause during play, then tap Choose a cat. Pick a cat and tap the play button to return to the room.
Why can’t I hear the bubble pops?
Check that Sound is on in the pause menu. Check your device’s volume and Silent Mode, too.
Can I play offline?
Yes. You can play the whole game without an internet connection or an account.
